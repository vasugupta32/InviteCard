Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

Because each invite is generated instantly and personalized to the details you submit, we are generally unable to offer refunds once an invite has been unlocked and delivered.

When a refund applies

  • You were charged but never received your unlocked invite/shareable link due to a technical error on our end.
  • You were double-charged for the same order.

How to request one

Email us within 48 hours of your payment via the details on our Contact Us page, including your payment ID (shown in your Razorpay receipt). Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.

Cancellations

Since delivery is instant, orders cannot be cancelled once payment is completed.
