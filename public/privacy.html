Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

InviteCraft ("we", "us") operates this website. This page explains what information we collect when you use it and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Details you enter into the invite form: host name(s), event date/time, venue, and your personal message. These are used only to generate your invite.
  • Payment information: we do not see or store your card, UPI, or bank details. Payments are processed entirely by Razorpay, our payment gateway partner, under their own security and privacy standards.
  • Basic technical data (IP address, browser type) collected automatically by our hosting provider for security and debugging.

How we use it

Solely to generate your personalized invite, process your payment, and respond if you contact us for support. We do not sell or rent your information to third parties.

Data retention

Invite details are kept only as long as needed to deliver your order and handle any support/refund request, after which they may be deleted.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see our Contact Us page.
